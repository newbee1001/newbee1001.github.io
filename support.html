Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new scent record?

Tap the "+" button on the Spaces tab. You'll be guided through a simple three-step process to record the room, scent details, and your personal notes.

Can I organize my records into collections?

Yes! Go to the Moments tab and tap the "+" icon to create a new collection. You can select multiple scent records to group together and give them a meaningful title.

How do I change the color theme?

Open Settings and scroll to the Appearance section. You'll find multiple color themes to choose from — each one updates the entire app's look instantly.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping regular device backups through your system's built-in backup feature to ensure your records are safe.

How do I delete a record?

Long-press on any scent card in the Spaces tab to reveal the context menu, then select "Delete." You can also delete records from their detail view.

Can I export my data?

Currently, data export is not supported. Your records are securely stored on your device and can be preserved through device backups.

What happens if I clear all data?

Going to Settings > Clear All Data will permanently remove all your scent records, collections, and preferences. This action cannot be undone.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Tfzqs works entirely offline. No internet connection is needed for any feature.
